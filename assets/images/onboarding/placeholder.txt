The following image files are needed in this directory:

1. city-skyline.png - A stylized city skyline transitioning from grayscale to vibrant colors
2. citizen-voice.png - Illustration of diverse citizens with speech bubbles forming a city landmark
3. character-evolution.png - Animation of character evolving through stages as it runs
4. game-mechanics.png - Split-screen showing character running and a survey question

These images should be created by a designer with the following specifications:
- Dimensions: approx. 800x600px
- Format: PNG with transparency
- Style: Flat, modern vector art with vibrant colors (teal, orange, green)

- un-habitat-logo.png - The official UN-Habitat logo
